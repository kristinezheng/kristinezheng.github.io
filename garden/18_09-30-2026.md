## overconsumption

Too spicy. Too rich. In the past two years, I've been getting heartburn everytime I eat something that is too oily and/or spicy. Even food that seems innocuous like ramen from a restaurant or small spoons of chili oil (which my tongue can handle) leads to hours of bloating and burning in my stomach.  

The world reminds me there are consequences to my gluttony. I should probably get that checked out. 

Beyond food, my consumption of social media and information has lasting effects: I am not able to reflect on what I consume nor am I spending time to respond/creatively express myself.
